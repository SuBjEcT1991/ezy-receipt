Delete Your Ezy Receipt Account

Ezy Receipt is developed by SuBjEcT and provides optional Ezy Cloud functionality. You can use this page to request deletion of your Ezy Cloud account and associated cloud data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Ezy Receipt / Ezy Cloud account, send an email to:

seifaltayar@gmail.com

Use the subject line: Ezy Receipt Account Deletion Request

Please send the request from the email address associated with your Ezy Cloud account so that ownership of the account can be verified.

What will be deleted?

Following a verified account deletion request, we will delete the Ezy Cloud account and associated cloud data under our control, which may include:

  • Ezy Cloud account information
  • Cloud-synchronised business profiles
  • Cloud-synchronised customer records
  • Cloud-synchronised receipts and receipt information
  • Cloud-synchronised settings
  • Supported attachments stored through Ezy Cloud

Information stored on your device

Deleting an Ezy Cloud account does not automatically remove information stored locally on your phone, tablet or computer.

Local Ezy Receipt information can be removed using the app's available deletion controls or by clearing/uninstalling the application from the device.

Retention

Account deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period. Certain information may be retained where necessary to comply with applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory obligations. Any retained information will only be kept for as long as required for those purposes.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once the Ezy Cloud account and associated cloud data have been deleted, that information may not be recoverable.
