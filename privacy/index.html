Ezy Receipt Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026

Ezy Receipt is a receipt and customer-management application created by SuBjEcT. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Ezy Receipt.

1. Information you enter into Ezy Receipt

Ezy Receipt allows you to enter and store information required to create and manage receipts. This may include business details, customer names and contact information, addresses, receipt and transaction information, payment status, work descriptions, notes, and attachments or photographic evidence.

This information is provided by you for the purpose of using the app's functionality.

2. Information stored on your device

Ezy Receipt is designed so that core app functionality can be used locally. Receipt information, customers, business profiles, preferences and related information may therefore be stored on your device.

Ezy Receipt does not require you to create an Ezy Cloud account simply to create and manage local receipts.

3. Ezy Cloud

Ezy Receipt includes an optional feature called Ezy Cloud.

If you choose to use Ezy Cloud, an account may be created using information such as your email address. Data required to provide cloud synchronisation may then be transmitted to and stored using our cloud infrastructure.

Ezy Cloud may synchronise information including business profiles, customers, receipts, settings and supported receipt attachments between compatible devices.

Cloud authentication and data-storage services are currently provided using Supabase infrastructure.

Ezy Cloud is optional. You may use supported local functionality without enabling cloud synchronisation.

4. Customer information

Ezy Receipt may contain personal information relating to your customers because you choose to enter that information when producing and managing receipts.

Depending on how you use the app, this may include a customer's name, address, telephone number, email address, transaction information and notes.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have an appropriate reason and, where required, permission to collect and process information relating to your customers.

Ezy Receipt does not independently obtain this customer information for advertising purposes.

5. Contacts

Ezy Receipt may offer functionality that allows you to import customer information from contacts available on your device.

Access to contacts is requested only when required for supported contact-import functionality and subject to the permissions provided by your device.

Ezy Receipt does not use your contacts for advertising.

6. Photos, files and attachments

You may choose to attach photographs, receipts, evidence or other supported files to records within Ezy Receipt.

These files may remain locally on your device or, where supported and where Ezy Cloud functionality is used, may be uploaded to cloud storage to provide synchronisation functionality.

Ezy Receipt does not access photographs or files unrelated to functionality you choose to use.

7. Device security and biometrics

Ezy Receipt may allow supported device-security features, such as biometric authentication or your device credential, to protect access to the app.

Biometric verification is performed using security functionality provided by your operating system. Ezy Receipt does not receive or store your fingerprint or facial biometric data.

8. Sharing and exporting

Ezy Receipt allows you to generate, download, export and share information such as receipt PDFs and business records.

When you choose to share information using another application or service, the information you select is passed to that service at your request. The receiving service's own privacy policy will apply to its handling of that information.

9. How information is used

Information processed by Ezy Receipt is used to provide the app's functionality, including creating and managing receipts, managing customers and business profiles, recording payment information, generating documents, providing backups or exports, and providing optional cloud synchronisation.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

10. Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by Ezy Receipt and Ezy Cloud. However, no electronic storage or internet transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should protect access to your device and your Ezy Cloud credentials.

11. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information can be managed through functionality provided by Ezy Receipt, including supported deletion features.

Information synchronised using Ezy Cloud may remain in cloud storage while your account and associated data remain active or where retention is reasonably required to provide the service.

Where applicable, users may request deletion of their Ezy Cloud account and associated personal data by contacting us using the details below.

12. Children's privacy

Ezy Receipt is intended as a business and productivity application and is not designed for children.

We do not knowingly design Ezy Receipt to collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

13. Third-party services

Ezy Receipt may use third-party infrastructure or operating-system services required to provide its functionality. These may include Supabase for optional cloud authentication, database and storage functionality, and Google/Android services associated with distribution and operation of the Android application.

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Ezy Receipt develops or when its data-processing practices change. Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy and its effective date.
